BREAST HISTOPATHOLOGY

Cancer & Tissue Information

Histopathological categories included in BreastCare AI.

CATEGORY 01

Benign Conditions

BENIGN
Adenosis

A benign breast condition involving enlarged or increased breast glands.

Fibroadenoma

A common benign breast tumor containing glandular and connective tissue.

Phyllodes Tumor

A fibroepithelial breast tumor that may be benign, borderline, or malignant.

Tubular Adenoma

A rare benign breast tumor characterized by tightly packed tubular structures.

CATEGORY 02

Malignant Conditions

MALIGNANT
Ductal Carcinoma

A carcinoma associated with the ducts of the breast. Diagnosis requires pathological evaluation.

Lobular Carcinoma

A carcinoma associated with the lobular structures of the breast.

Mucinous Carcinoma

A breast carcinoma characterized by tumor cells associated with extracellular mucin.

Papillary Carcinoma

A breast lesion characterized by papillary growth patterns.

Educational Information

The information presented here is intended for educational and research purposes. Histopathological diagnosis should be performed and interpreted by qualified healthcare professionals.
