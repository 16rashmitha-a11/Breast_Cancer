Understanding Breast Cancer

Breast cancer is a disease in which abnormal cells in the breast grow and multiply uncontrollably. It can develop in different parts of the breast and may have different characteristics. Early detection and appropriate medical evaluation can help identify breast abnormalities and support timely treatment.
